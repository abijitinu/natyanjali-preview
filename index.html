From your first step to the stage

Bharatanatyam is learned in stages, and every student starts with the basics.

  1. 01 Vidyarambam The traditional first day of learning, and the day our newest batches began.
  2. 02 Adavu The basic units of movement: footwork, posture and rhythm, starting from the half-seated araimandi.
  3. 03 Hasta The hand gestures, or mudras, that let a dancer tell a story without words.
  4. 04 Abhinaya Expression through the face and body, bringing meaning and feeling to the dance.
  5. 05 Margam The traditional sequence of a recital, from Alarippu through to Tillana.
  6. 06 Arangettam A dancer's first full performance on stage, and a milestone our students have reached.
