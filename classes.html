Good to know

Fees, demos and missed classes

Fees
Fees are monthly and depend on the location and the student's age. Events have a separate fee. Please send us an enquiry for the current fees.
Demo classes
You are welcome to sit in and watch a class before you join. Online demo classes are also available. Just ask us.
Missed classes
There is no refund for missed classes, but they can be made up by attending another batch. No fee is charged for a month in which the student attends no classes.
Health
Please tell us about any injury or health condition before joining, so we can plan the classes safely.
